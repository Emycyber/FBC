How to Gamble Safely

Most people who bet do so without any serious problems. The following habits help keep gambling in its proper place as entertainment rather than a financial strategy.

  • Set a weekly or monthly gambling budget and never exceed it.
  • Only bet money you can afford to lose completely.
  • Never borrow money to gamble.
  • Do not chase losses. A losing streak does not guarantee a win is coming.
  • Take regular breaks. Do not bet every single day.
  • Keep gambling separate from your financial obligations — rent, food, bills.
  • Bet as singles rather than accumulators to reduce risk per session.
  • Do not gamble when you are upset, stressed or under the influence of alcohol.

Signs of Problem Gambling

Problem gambling does not always look obvious — even to the person experiencing it. The following signs may indicate that gambling has become a problem:

  • Spending more money on gambling than you planned or can afford.
  • Chasing losses — continuing to bet to try to recover money you have lost.
  • Hiding gambling activity from family or friends.
  • Borrowing money or selling possessions to fund gambling.
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or restless when you are not gambling.
  • Gambling affecting your work, studies or relationships.
  • Gambling as a way to escape stress, depression or personal problems.
  • Repeated unsuccessful attempts to cut back or stop gambling.

If you recognise any of these signs in yourself or someone you know, please reach out for help. Problem gambling is a recognised condition and effective support is available.

Self-Exclusion

If you feel you need to stop gambling, self-exclusion is one of the most effective tools available. Most major betting platforms allow you to temporarily or permanently close your account. You can usually find this option under account settings or by contacting customer support directly.

On platforms like Sportybet, Bet9ja, 1xBet and Betwinner you can request account closure or a cooling-off period through their live chat or support email. We encourage you to use these tools without hesitation if you feel your gambling is getting out of control.

Setting Deposit and Stake Limits

Many betting platforms allow you to set daily, weekly or monthly deposit limits directly on your account. Setting these limits before you start betting — not after you have already lost money — is one of the most practical steps you can take to protect yourself. Once set, responsible platforms enforce a waiting period before any increase takes effect, which gives you time to reconsider. Check your account settings on your preferred platform to see what limit options are available.

Where to Get Help

The following organisations offer free, confidential support for problem gambling. You do not need to be in crisis to reach out — early help is always better.

BeGambleAware

Free confidential support and advice for anyone affected by gambling.

begambleaware.org

Helpline: 0808 8020 133 (free, 24/7)

Gamblers Anonymous

A fellowship of people who share their experience to help each other recover.

gamblersanonymous.org
GamCare

Provides information, advice and support for people affected by problem gambling.

gamcare.org.uk

Helpline: 0808 8020 133 (free, 24/7)

National Council on Problem Gambling

International resource for problem gambling support and treatment referrals.

ncpgambling.org

Helpline: 1-800-522-4700
